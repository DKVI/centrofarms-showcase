Description

Himalayan Pink Salt is a natural rock salt known for its distinctive pink color and crystalline texture. Packed in coarse grains, it offers a balanced salty taste suitable for everyday cooking, seasoning, and finishing dishes before serving.

The clear bottle makes it easy to monitor the remaining salt, while the secure cap provides convenient storage and everyday use. The salt can be used with meats, seafood, vegetables, salads, soups, eggs, and a wide variety of other dishes.

Key Features:

  • Natural rock salt with a distinctive pink color
  • Coarse crystals suitable for grinding
  • Balanced salty taste for versatile everyday use
  • Ideal for cooking, seasoning, and finishing
  • Clear, convenient packaging for easy storage
  • Net Weight: 368.5 g (13 oz)

Suitable for: Home kitchens, restaurants, food service businesses, professional kitchens, catering services, and food retailers.

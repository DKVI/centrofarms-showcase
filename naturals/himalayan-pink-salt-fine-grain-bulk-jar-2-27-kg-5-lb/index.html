Himalayan Pink Salt Fine Grain Bulk Jar 2.27 kg (5 lb)

Himalayan Pink Salt Fine Grain is packed in a large 2.27 kg (5 lb) jar, making it a practical choice for frequent use in home kitchens, restaurants, and professional food service environments. The salt features a naturally distinctive pink color and a fine grain texture that blends easily with ingredients and distributes evenly during cooking.

The fine grain format is ready to use without additional grinding, making it suitable for seasoning, marinating meats and seafood, preparing soups, sauces, salads, vegetables, grilled dishes, and finishing meals before serving.

The large jar with a secure screw-top lid provides convenient storage and easy access, especially for kitchens with higher daily usage.

Key Features:

  • Himalayan pink salt in fine grain form
  • Naturally distinctive pink color
  • Blends easily and distributes evenly in food
  • Ready to use without grinding
  • Large format for frequent or professional use
  • Convenient bulk jar with secure lid
  • Net Weight: 2.27 kg (5 lb)

Suitable for: Home kitchens, restaurants, professional kitchens, catering services, food service businesses, and food retailers.

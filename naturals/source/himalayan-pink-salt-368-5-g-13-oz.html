Description

Description

Himalayan Pink Salt is a natural rock salt known for its distinctive pink color, formed from ancient salt deposits in the Himalayan region. It has a mild, balanced salty taste and is easy to use in a wide variety of everyday dishes, including seasoning, marinating meat, preparing seafood, salads, sauces, or as a finishing salt before serving.

Its natural pink color comes from the presence of trace minerals within the salt crystals. The product is packed in a clear jar with a secure screw-top lid, making it convenient to store while helping protect the salt from moisture during everyday use.

Key Features:

  • Natural rock salt with a distinctive pink color
  • Mild, balanced salty taste suitable for a variety of dishes
  • Ideal for cooking, seasoning, marinating, and finishing
  • Naturally colored crystals that add visual appeal to food
  • Secure jar packaging for convenient storage and daily use

Suitable for: Home kitchens, restaurants, food service businesses, professional kitchens, and food retailers.

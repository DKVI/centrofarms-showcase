THE INVESTMENT THESIS

Building Long-Term Wealth
Through American Farmland

CentroFarms™ gives investors an opportunity to build long-term wealth through ownership of income-producing agricultural real estate in the United States.

Through our Build Together investment model, we partner with private investors and farming families to acquire, modernize, and expand commercial poultry operations. By combining established poultry production agreements with real estate ownership and efficient farm management, we aim to generate operating income, pay down debt, and build equity over time.

Our approach is designed to create more than a poultry operation. It provides a foundation for long-term asset ownership, potential expansion into complementary agricultural businesses, and opportunities for families looking to establish an active business presence in the United States.

HOW THE MODEL WORKS

Operate with purpose
Build equity over time

01

Operating Income Through Poultry Production

A production model designed to limit major cost exposure

Feed, chicks, and veterinary care are among the largest costs in poultry production (approximately 70%). Under our proposed contract production structure, the production partner covers 100% of these expenses, allowing the farm to focus on facility ownership, maintenance, utilities, labor, and overall operational performance.

Recurring production cycles

Commercial poultry houses operate through repeated production cycles. With suitable contracts and consistent performance, these cycles can support recurring operating revenue and help the business meet its financing obligations.

Demand for an essential product

Poultry is a staple protein in the American food market. While demand provides a strong foundation for the industry, profitability still depends on production agreements, operating efficiency, financing costs, and other market conditions.

02

Building Equity Through Land and Infrastructure

Ownership of tangible assets

Investors participate in a business built around physical assets, including agricultural land, commercial poultry houses, and production equipment. Ownership rights and collateral arrangements depend on the project's legal structure and financing documents.

Using operating income to pay down debt

Our model is designed to use farm operating income to cover expenses, service debt, and gradually reduce outstanding loan balances. As debt is repaid, the business may build additional equity in its land and facilities, subject to property values and the terms of its financing.

Where appropriate, we will evaluate USDA, SBA, and other financing programs to determine which options are available for each project.

A long-term asset for future generations

Agricultural real estate can provide a foundation for long-term family wealth and future business expansion. Property appreciation, tax treatment, and depreciation benefits depend on market conditions, ownership structure, and applicable tax rules; none should be assumed as a guaranteed outcome.

03

Opportunities for International Investors

An active operating business

CentroFarms™ develops and operates commercial agricultural businesses rather than relying solely on passive property ownership. This operating structure may be relevant to international investors exploring U.S. business and immigration opportunities.

Evaluating L-1, E-2 and EB-5 pathways

Depending on the investor's nationality, investment amount, ownership structure, job creation, and other requirements, a qualifying project may offer a potential pathway under the L-1 Intracompany Transferee, E-2 Treaty Investor, or EB-5 Immigrant Investor program.

Rural EB-5 projects may qualify for specific investment thresholds and visa set-asides when they meet the applicable legal requirements. However, agricultural land or a rural location alone does not establish eligibility. Each project must be evaluated with qualified immigration counsel before any visa-related representations are made.

A transparent, asset-backed business

We aim to give investors a clear understanding of the business, including its real estate, operating agreements, financing, expenses, and performance. Our goal is to make the investment structure understandable from the outset, with clear reporting and defined responsibilities for each partner.

CentroFarms™ offers multiple physical formats to accommodate diverse field application methods, custom blending requirements, and commercial handling systems:

01

Dried Fly Granules (DFG)

Natural, unground material suited for bulk soil pre-plant incorporation, raw compost amendment blending, and base broadcast applications where particle variability is acceptable.

02

Crumbles

Sized, fractured granules engineered for consistent volumetric distribution and rapid breakdown upon contact with soil moisture, making them ideal for top-dressing, bed preparation, and nursery container media.

03

Cylindrical Pellets

Dense, extruded cylindrical pellets engineered for low-dust handling and uniform flow through standard mechanical hoppers, gravity drops, and banded placement equipment, dissolving gradually for steady nutrient release.

04

Spherical Granules

Precision-calibrated, free-flowing spherical granules specifically designed for seamless calibration through commercial spin spreaders, pneumatic air-boom applicators, and automated bulk blending lines.

Particle size distribution (SGN), bulk density, moisture levels, and spreader calibration guidelines are available upon request and confirmed on each lot's Certificate of Analysis (CoA).

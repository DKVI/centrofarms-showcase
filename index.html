Organic Fertilizer

Increased Yields ● Pest and Disease Suppression ● Control of Nematodes & Broad-Spectrum Pathogens

EnviroPro is more than a fertilizer – it's a biologically active soil amendment rich in nutrients, chitin, and beneficial microbial communities. The naturally occurring chitin in BSFL frass confers functional properties that stimulate chitosan production, supporting plant defenses against disease-causing pathogens and plant-parasitic nematodes. These biological interactions help optimize soil health, nutrient cycling, root quality, and overall turf vigor.

Biological Soil Interactions

Supporting Beneficial Soil Microbes

Chitin and other organic matter in frass are studied for their interactions with soil organisms and plant–microbe relationships.

A biological component

Chitin from shed exoskeletons

Chitin is a structural component of insect exoskeletons. In soil, chitin-rich amendments can interact with microbial communities and plant defense signaling. Responses vary with the amendment, soil and growing conditions; this does not mean a specific microorganism is present in every product.

Core Takeaways

Black soldier fly larvae frass is a recycled soil amendment that can return organic matter and nutrients to agricultural soils. Depending on the product and conditions, it may support soil structure, nutrient cycling and plant–soil interactions as part of a broader sustainable farming approach.

Circular agricultureSoil healthResource recovery
01

Organic Matter for Soil

Frass adds organic material to soil. Its effects on soil structure, water retention and nutrient availability depend on the product, soil and application.

02

Nutrients & Bioactive Components

Provides nutrients alongside naturally occurring components such as chitin. Use the product-specific guaranteed analysis and current label for application decisions.

03

Part of Integrated Crop Care

Frass and its components are being studied for interactions with soil biology and plant defenses. It is not a substitute for crop-specific fertility or pest management.

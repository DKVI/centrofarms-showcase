Designed For Commercial
Poultry Operations

CentroFarms™ Equipment Systems is intended for farm owners, poultry operators, agricultural contractors, and facility developers looking for practical infrastructure solutions.

  • Farm owners
  • Poultry operators
  • Agricultural contractors
  • Facility developers

Product selection should be based on facility dimensions, animal requirements, expected loads, sanitation procedures, and compatibility with existing equipment.

Our Approach

We focus on practical engineering, material selection, and system compatibility. Rather than treating each component as a standalone product, we consider how flooring, framing, connections, and facility operations work together.

The objective is to help commercial operators build facilities that are easier to maintain, suited to their operating conditions, and designed for long-term use.

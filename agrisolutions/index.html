Discover sustainable nutrition with
EnviroProducts

EnviroBug

EnviroBug is a nutritious, premium ingredient composed of oven-dried whole Black Soldier Fly Larvae. Benefits: a single source of amino acids and fatty acids, high in protein, sustainable, and a natural food item.

EnviroMeal

EnviroMeal is created by pressing fat out of dried black soldier fly larvae (BSFL), making a high-protein, finely ground product. Benefits include high digestibility and naturally occurring lauric and amino acids.

EnviroOil

EnviroOil is derived from pressed dried black soldier fly larvae, resulting in an oil rich in lauric acid, comparable to coconut or palm oil. Lauric acid is a nutrient known for its antimicrobial properties. Benefits include potential protection against unhealthy gut bacteria and high resistance to oxidation.

EnviroPro

EnviroPro is an OMRI-listed and CDFA-registered soil amendment and natural nutrient source. Proudly crafted in the USA, it is a sustainable co-product of EnviroFlight’s premium black soldier fly larvae production system. By feeding the soil ecosystem, EnviroPro delivers a holistic, all-natural input designed to restore soil health and maximize crop performance.

EnviroFeed

EnviroFeed is a nutrient-rich product derived from the production of BSFL. As a feed ingredient, it contains similar benefits to those of all our EnviroProducts. In addition to lauric acid, protein and carbohydrates, EnviroFeed also carries value-added benefits such as antimicrobial peptides and chitin.

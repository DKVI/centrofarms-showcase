EnviroBug

EnviroBug is EnviroFlight’s whole, oven-dried black soldier fly larvae (Hermetia illucens): a protein-rich ingredient supplying both amino acids and fatty acids.

Product Overview

Whole Larvae
One Ingredient

EnviroFlight ® | Darling Ingredients

EnviroBug keeps the larvae in their whole dried form. Unlike the pressed meal and separated oil, it brings the protein and lipid fractions together.

EnviroFlight describes oven drying without chemicals or microwaves. Ask for the current technical sheet and batch analysis when evaluating a formulation.

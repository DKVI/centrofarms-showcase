EnviroFeed

EnviroFeed is a nutrient-containing co-product of EnviroFlight’s black soldier fly larvae production, developed as an ingredient for animal feed.

Explore EnviroFeed ↓
Product Overview

More Value From
The Larvae System

EnviroFeed is the frass-based feed co-product from BSFL production. Frass includes residual feed material, larval excretions and shed exoskeletons; its composition depends on the feedstock and production process.

The manufacturer describes protein, carbohydrates and lauric acid, alongside chitin and antimicrobial peptides. Assess these components using current product specifications.

Research In Practice

What The Studies Report

2024

Broiler Diets

Two experiments compared balanced diets with and without BSFL frass. Feed intake, weight gain and feed conversion did not differ significantly under the tested conditions.

Adams & Koutsos · 2024 ↗
2025

Nursery Pig Diets

A six-week conference study evaluated increasing frass inclusion. Growth performance and diarrhea incidence were not significantly affected in the tested diets.

Rubio et al · 2025 · Conference Abstract ↗

Results apply to the specific frass source, diets and trial conditions. Research inclusion levels are not general feeding directions or guaranteed performance improvements.

EnviroOil

EnviroOil is the lipid fraction pressed from dried black soldier fly larvae. It brings an insect-derived source of fat and energy to animal feed formulations.

Explore EnviroOil ↓
Hermetia illucens EnviroFlight ® / Darling Ingredients
Product Overview

A Different Source
Of Dietary Energy

EnviroFlight identifies EnviroOil as a lauric-acid-rich oil. Evaluate the fatty acid profile alongside the energy and processing requirements of your formula.

EnviroOil is the separated oil fraction; EnviroMeal is the protein-rich meal after pressing, while EnviroBug retains the whole dried larvae format.

Research In Practice

Studied In Animal Nutrition

01 / Nursery Pigs

Growth & Feed Efficiency

A 40-day study published in 2022 evaluated BSFL oil replacing corn oil in nursery pig diets. The authors reported improved daily gain and feed efficiency under the trial conditions.

van Heugten et al · 2022 ↗
02 / Adult Dogs

Acceptance & Digestibility

A 2021 study tested BSFL oil in extruded diets for adult dogs. Diets were accepted and nutrient digestibility and general health were maintained within the study conditions.

Freel et al · 2021 ↗

Research outcomes depend on the species, formulation and trial conditions. Study inclusion levels are not universal feeding directions.

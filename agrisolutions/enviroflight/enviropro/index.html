Healthy soil is essential to long-term agricultural productivity. However, intensive cropping and certain fertilizer management practices can reduce soil organic matter and affect the biological activity that supports plant growth.

CentroFarms™ Plant Nutrition focuses on black soldier fly larvae (BSFL) frass, an insect-derived biological input for circular, resource-efficient agriculture.

EPro 3-2-1
Organic Frass

EnviroFlight ® | Darling Ingredients

EPro 3-2-1 is our featured frass-based soil amendment, designed for commercial growers seeking an additional source of nutrients and organic material for their soil management programs.

By turning insect-rearing byproducts into agricultural inputs, we aim to connect waste recovery with practical solutions for commercial farming.

FROM INSECT REARING TO AGRICULTURAL INPUTS

Available Commercial Formats

CentroFarms™ offers multiple physical formats to accommodate diverse field application methods, custom blending requirements, and commercial handling systems:

01

Dried Fly Granules (DFG)

Natural, unground material suited for bulk soil pre-plant incorporation, raw compost amendment blending, and base broadcast applications where particle variability is acceptable.

02

Crumbles

Sized, fractured granules engineered for consistent volumetric distribution and rapid breakdown upon contact with soil moisture, making them ideal for top-dressing, bed preparation, and nursery container media.

03

Cylindrical Pellets

Dense, extruded cylindrical pellets engineered for low-dust handling and uniform flow through standard mechanical hoppers, gravity drops, and banded placement equipment, dissolving gradually for steady nutrient release.

04

Spherical Granules

Precision-calibrated, free-flowing spherical granules specifically designed for seamless calibration through commercial spin spreaders, pneumatic air-boom applicators, and automated bulk blending lines.

Particle size distribution (SGN), bulk density, moisture levels, and spreader calibration guidelines are available upon request and confirmed on each lot's Certificate of Analysis (CoA).
COMMERCIAL APPLICATIONS & AGRONOMIC TRIALS

Field Results

EPro 3-2-1 delivers proven bio-fertility, soil conditioning, and root-zone stimulation across intensive horticulture and perennial systems. Combining balanced macronutrients (~3.6% N, ~2% P, ~1% K) with natural chitin, organic carbon, and beneficial microbes, it drives measurable yield and disease resilience.

Tomatoes

Up to +135%

Documented studies show yield increases of up to 135% when frass is combined with reduced rates of synthetic fertilizer, delivering strong synergistic nutrient uptake and root development.

Corn

+7–27%

Consistently outperforms conventional inputs, generating 7% to 27% higher grain yields compared to urea and standard organic fertilizers.

Vegetables & Leafy Greens (Lettuce)

About +50%

Boosted commercial yields by approximately 50% over conventional fertilizer regimens.

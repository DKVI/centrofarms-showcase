EnviroMeal

EnviroMeal is a finely ground black soldier fly larvae ingredient from EnviroFlight, produced by pressing part of the fat from dried larvae.

Explore EnviroMeal ↓

Product Overview

Insect Protein
For Your Formulation

EnviroFlight describes EnviroMeal as a protein-rich, palatable and digestible ingredient that supplies amino acids. Its fine meal format differs from EnviroBug’s whole dried larvae and EnviroOil’s separated lipid fraction.

Evaluate it as part of a balanced formula, using the current specification sheet for protein, residual fat, moisture and amino acid composition.

Research In Practice

Evidence For Formulation
Decisions

01 / Adult Dogs

Digestibility & Acceptance

A 2021 study evaluated partially defatted BSFL meal in extruded diets for adult dogs. Acceptance and nutrient digestibility were maintained under the tested conditions.

Freel et al · 2021 ↗
02 / Atlantic Salmon

Alternative Protein Blends

A 12-week trial tested EnviroMeal with fermented soy protein in juvenile Atlantic salmon diets. Growth outcomes were similar across the tested diets; the findings concern the blend and trial conditions.

Fredrickson et al · 2023 ↗

Study formulations are not feeding directions. Choose inclusion levels with a qualified nutritionist and current product specifications.
